# Asset Naming Conventions

When moving or adding images/PDFs to `assets/`:

- Use kebab-case: `email-deliverability-guide-2022.pdf`
- Include year or context when relevant
- Avoid spaces, special characters, or "Untitled"
- Keep originals in place until the move is verified — do not delete until the new location is confirmed and referenced

## Examples

| Old | Suggested New |
|-----|---------------|
| Untitled document.pdf | research-untitled-notes.pdf (or better descriptive name) |
| Screen+Shot+2018-03-30....png | screenshot-email-ui-2018.png |
| 1.pdf – 6.pdf | identify content and rename accordingly |

Update any article references after moving.
