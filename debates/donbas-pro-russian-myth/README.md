# Donbas debate notes — "pro-Russian Donbas caused the occupation" myth

Debate brief (Peterson/Shapiro style) prepared for Arthur Tkachenko (3 November 2026). About 10 pages. Topic: debunking the claim that a "pro-Russian Donbas" caused the 2014 occupation / war.

## Key numbers (from the notes)

- **1991 independence referendum:** Donetsk **83.90%** YES, Luhansk **83.86%** YES (of votes cast).
- **Miners' strikes:** July **1989** (Donbas joined the all-Union strike; 173 of 226 Donbas collieries) and March **1991** (Ukrainian miners demanded constitutional status for Ukraine's Declaration of State Sovereignty).
