# Image Tips

- Prefer clear, well-lit photos that match the story.
- 1–3 images per post is usually enough.
- Copy files into the post folder; do not delete or move the originals from the dump yet.
- If you are unsure, leave the image in the dump and decide later.
- Use `LI/images/INDEX.md` to browse quickly.
- After you copy images, update `status-tracker.md` so you know the post is closer to ready.
