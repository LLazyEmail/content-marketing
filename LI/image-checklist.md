# Image Checklist

Use this every time you pick images so you stop grabbing randomly.

1. Open the post folder you are working on.
2. Read the text in `post.md`.
3. Open `LI/images/INDEX.md` and look only for photos that match the story.
4. Copy (do not move) the matching files into the post’s `images/` folder.
5. Aim for 1–4 images. Leave the rest in the dump.
6. Mark "images chosen" in `status-tracker.md` or `queue.md`.
7. When both text and images are ready, publish and add the link to `published.md`.

Stay on one post until the checklist is done. Then move to the next.
