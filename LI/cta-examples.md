# CTA Examples for LinkedIn Posts

Good closers that invite engagement:

- What’s the smallest system that has kept your team moving when motivation ran out?
- Who on your team quietly carries the heaviest load?
- Have you ever had to reverse-engineer a fix because the official supply chain failed?
- What story from your own experience still hits the same way years later?
- Drop a comment if you’ve worked with volunteers who became part of the system.

Pick one that fits the story. End with a clear question or invitation.
