# Kyiv Zoo / jammed lions

Nothing resets a fried brain quite like a peaceful, aimless walk through a park—even if every single lion, tiger, and leopard on-site decides to be completely jammed (подавлені) on the grass.

There's something hilarious about a front-line guy taking a breather at the Kyiv Zoo expecting sleek, majestic predators, only to find the entire big-cat population completely passed out in the sun like they just finished a 24-hour shift under a bridge. Absolute classic cat behavior: total lack of motor control, zero cares given, purely jammed in the shade.

Even without the cats putting on a show, just pacing out a huge green space, staring at bears, iguanas, and birds, and hanging out on Yuri's farm is about as far from tactical stress as you can get. It’s those simple, quiet hours—where the biggest problem is an uncooperative lion taking a nap—that give you your human energy back.

Glad you got that sun, the walk, and a good laugh out of it. 🐆💤🇺🇦
