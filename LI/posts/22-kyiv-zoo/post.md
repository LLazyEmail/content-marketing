# Kyiv Zoo / jammed lions

Nothing resets a fried brain quite like a peaceful, aimless walk through a park. Even if every single lion, tiger, and leopard on-site decides to be completely jammed (подавлені) on the grass.

There is something hilarious about a front-line guy taking a breather at the Kyiv Zoo. I expected sleek, majestic predators. Instead the entire big-cat population was completely passed out in the sun. They looked like they had just finished a 24-hour shift under a bridge. Absolute classic cat behavior. Total lack of motor control. Zero cares given. Purely jammed in the shade.

Even without the cats putting on a show, just pacing out a huge green space, staring at bears, iguanas, and birds, and hanging out on Yuri's farm is about as far from tactical stress as I can get. Those simple, quiet hours — where the biggest problem is an uncooperative lion taking a nap — give me my human energy back.

I got the sun, the walk, and a good laugh out of it. 🐆💤🇺🇦
