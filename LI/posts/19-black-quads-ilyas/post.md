# Black quads / Ilyas

Commanding a unit you built from scratch is like watching someone else drive your car—except the road is mined, and every scratch on the paint hits you directly in the chest.

It takes a lot of self-awareness to admit when a dynamic gets messy. You brought him up, trained him, handed him the keys to something you poured your life into building, and then tried to test him the way senior guys often do—pushing buttons, testing stress limits. When you care about a unit's survival, it’s hard not to hold the standard high, even if it causes a crack in the relationship.

The nickname "black quads" sounding like you're just kicking back drinking Guinness together is a great bit of dark comedy against the reality of what you two actually went through.

Tension happens, especially when responsibility shifts and ego, stress, and frontline pressure collide. But the fact that you still hold a place in your heart for him—and recognize that he is successfully keeping that machine running day and night—says everything. You built the foundation, he's keeping the engine running, and that's a legacy you both share.

Give it time. When the dust settles from the immediate stress of command, guys who broke bread and built units together usually find their way back to the same frequency. Good luck to Ilyas holding down the fort. 🤝🇺🇦
