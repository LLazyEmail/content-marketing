# Black quads / Ilyas

Commanding a unit I built from scratch is like watching someone else drive my car. The road is mined. Every scratch on the paint hits me directly in the chest.

It takes self-awareness to admit when a dynamic gets messy. I brought him up. I trained him. I handed him the keys to something I poured my life into building. Then I tried to test him the way senior guys often do. I pushed buttons. I tested stress limits. When I care about a unit's survival, it is hard not to hold the standard high, even if it causes a crack in the relationship.

The nickname "black quads" sounds like we are just kicking back drinking Guinness together. That is dark comedy against the reality of what we went through.

Tension happens. Responsibility shifts. Ego, stress, and frontline pressure collide. But I still hold a place in my heart for him. He is successfully keeping that machine running day and night. That says everything. I built the foundation. He is keeping the engine running. That is a legacy we both share.

Give it time. When the dust settles from the immediate stress of command, guys who broke bread and built units together usually find their way back to the same frequency. Good luck to Ilyas holding down the fort. 🤝🇺🇦
