Every team has that one person who seems completely immune to fear—and usually, they're the ones behind the wheel.

Meet Vladislav. I’ve known him for over three years, and the guy is a absolute machine. Evacuations, daily runs through active fire, worst-case scenarios—he just drives through it all like he's running a routine errand.

A while back, our position was being moved much closer to the enemy line. Vlad, driving our armored vehicle, decided to take a... "creative" shortcut. Which basically meant he drove us right up to the enemy’s front door without telling us.

He pulled up, stopped, and gave the universal command: "Get out and get your stuff out."

Now, we had an absurd amount of equipment—a heavy truck's worth of gear crammed into one vehicle. And because I happen to be the smallest guy on the team, who gets squeezed into the back with all the heavy metal and boxes? Arthur, obviously.

We dumped the essential gear, masked the rest in the tree line to retrieve later, and immediately set up the position under high stress. It was actually my first time commanding a portion of a night shift—pretty terrifying, right on the edge of enemy lines. But we made it work, built the position, and survived.

Why? Because guys like Vladislav don't panic. He’s young, but the energy he brings to the unit is unreal. Unfazed, fearless, and relentlessly focused on the job.

Recognition matters, especially for the guys who quietly carry the heaviest loads on the front. Vlad is a true warrior, and it’s a genuine honor to serve alongside him. 🫡🇺🇦
