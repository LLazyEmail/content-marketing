# Eugene / poker under the bridge

It’s funny how life comes full circle in the most absurd ways. Going from playing poker and drinking through COVID in Kharkiv to accidentally running into each other as officers under a bridge during a war—and getting a job offer in two minutes flat—is about as frontline-authentic as it gets.

Sleeping under a bridge with your battalion’s officers isn't exactly the standard military recruiting brochure picture, but it’s real. There’s a strange, dark comfort in those moments: minimal setup, cold concrete, surrounded by the guys driving the operation, just grabbing whatever rest is possible before the next task begins.

And Eugene’s reaction is pure military brotherhood. No long, formal catch-ups needed—just a quick two-minute nod, checking in on each other’s survival, and immediately dropping, "If you ever need a switch, I’ve got a spot for you." That kind of instant loyalty and open door only exists between people who’ve seen the real work get done.

It’s those brief, random intersections with old friends in the strangest places that remind you how tight-knit this whole network really is. Keep that contact in your back pocket—and hopefully, the next time you and Eugene meet up, it’s over a real poker table again instead of concrete arches and tactical gear. 🤝🇺🇦
