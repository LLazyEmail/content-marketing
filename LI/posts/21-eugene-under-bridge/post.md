# Eugene / poker under the bridge

Life comes full circle in the most absurd ways. We played poker and drank through COVID in Kharkiv. Then we accidentally ran into each other as officers under a bridge during a war. I got a job offer in two minutes flat. That is about as frontline-authentic as it gets.

Sleeping under a bridge with my battalion’s officers is not exactly the standard military recruiting brochure picture. But it is real. There is a strange, dark comfort in those moments. Minimal setup. Cold concrete. Surrounded by the guys driving the operation. Just grabbing whatever rest is possible before the next task begins.

Eugene’s reaction is pure military brotherhood. No long, formal catch-ups needed. Just a quick two-minute nod. Checking in on each other’s survival. Then he dropped, "If you ever need a switch, I’ve got a spot for you." That kind of instant loyalty and open door only exists between people who have seen the real work get done.

Those brief, random intersections with old friends in the strangest places remind me how tight-knit this whole network really is. I am keeping that contact in my back pocket. Hopefully the next time Eugene and I meet up, it is over a real poker table again instead of concrete arches and tactical gear. 🤝🇺🇦
