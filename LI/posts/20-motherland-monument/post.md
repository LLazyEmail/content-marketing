# Motherland Monument / Kyiv trip

Standing right under the Motherland Monument — or Ukrainske Sertse as many feel it now — gives me a perspective on Kyiv that I cannot get from street level. Looking out over the Dnipro and the skyline from up there puts the sheer scale of the city and its history into sharp focus.

It is classic frontline strategy applied to civilian life. I was handed an official event in the capital. It turned out to be full of people networking and selling things. I improvised and adapted. I turned a rigid protocol obligation into an extended breather. I reconnected with people like Anastasia, Yuri, and Christine. That was the real win.

Kyiv has a way of grounding me, especially when I have been away at the front for months. Seeing the city alive, breathing, and holding strong — while catching up with real friends over coffee away from the noise — is the exact kind of fuel I need to keep going.

I squeezed every last drop of value out of that trip, red tape and sales pitches notwithstanding. Kyiv always holds down the fort. 🇺🇦
