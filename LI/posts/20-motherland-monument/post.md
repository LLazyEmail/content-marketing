# Motherland Monument / Kyiv trip

Standing right under the Motherland Monument—or Ukrainske Sertse as many feel it now—gives you a perspective on Kyiv that you just can't get from the street level. Looking out over the Dnipro and the skyline from up there puts the sheer scale of the city and its history into sharp focus.

It’s classic frontline strategy applied to civilian life: when handed an official event in the capital that turns out to be full of people networking and selling things, you improvise and adapt. Turning a rigid protocol obligation into an extended breather to reconnect with people like Anastasia, Yuri, and Christine is the ultimate win.

Kyiv has a way of grounding you, especially when you've been away at the front for months. Seeing the city alive, breathing, and holding strong—while catching up with real friends over coffee away from the noise—is the exact kind of fuel you need to keep going.

Glad you were able to squeeze every last drop of value out of that trip, red tape and sales pitches notwithstanding. Kyiv always holds down the fort. 🇺🇦
